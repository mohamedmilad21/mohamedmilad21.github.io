Data & analytics Level 3

How I work

Six weeks, then two

We run Shape Up: a six-week build cycle followed by a two-week cooldown that ends at a betting table. Nothing gets built because someone shouted loudest — it gets bet on.

Shape Up · betting table · cycles

Discovery before pitching

Stakeholder requests land on a Discovery board. They become pitches only after there's a real problem, evidence, and a look at how others have solved it.

Linear · Coda · Outline

Instrument, then ship

Every experiment gets a dashboard before it gets a rollout percentage. If I can't see the number drop, I have no business turning the feature on.

Metabase · Firebase · Mixpanel

Reading shelf

Hacking Growth — read in fifteen days, then presented to the team. Activation and retention finally clicked.
Shape Up — fixed cycles, appetite over estimates, cut scope not quality.
Inspired — fall in love with the problem first.
Continuous Discovery Habits — interviews as a habit, not an event.
The Mom Test — ask about their life, not your idea.

Work with me

I'm open to conversations about product roles, mentoring, and building for emerging markets — especially products where cash, low connectivity and multilingual users are the default, not the edge case.
